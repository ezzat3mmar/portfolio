01 — About

Hardware and firmware, treated as one problem.

I'm a Telecommunications & Electronics Engineering student with a strong focus on embedded systems — designing solutions that seamlessly integrate hardware and software to reach high performance and efficiency. My work spans designing electronic circuits, programming microcontrollers, and integrating sensors and actuators into systems that are both intelligent and reliable.

Embedded systems development

Firmware for 32-bit microcontrollers like STM32 and ESP32, using bare-metal and RTOS (FreeRTOS) for direct sensor acquisition and motor control.

Hardware testing & debugging

Oscilloscopes, logic analyzers and multimeters to trace UART, I2C, SPI and CAN — diagnosing integration issues and confirming performance.

Custom driver design

Low-level HAL and device drivers for peripherals and external hardware, tuned for data throughput and memory footprint.

Academic foundation

3.55/4.0 GPA in B.Eng. Communications & Electronics Engineering at Tanta University — theory balanced with applied hardware projects.
